Frequently asked questions

What does ImageTextLab do?

ImageTextLab scans Shopify store images for missing or weak alt text, generates concise AI suggestions, and lets merchants write approved text back to Shopify.

Does it change images automatically?

No. Generated text appears in a review queue before merchants write updates back to Shopify.

Does it use protected customer data?

No. ImageTextLab focuses on product, file, collection, article, page, and image metadata. It does not request protected customer data.

How is billing handled?

Billing is handled through Shopify managed billing. Merchants confirm the selected plan in Shopify before charges begin.

How can I get support?

Email hakan.olcer@web.de with your store domain, the action you tried, and any visible error message.
